# Essentas App

Author: Neil Paul<br>
Updated: 11/12/2026

This will be an app designed to reduce food waste at home by allowing users to quickly log and
inventory all of the food items in their fridge, freezer, and pantry. The app will estimate when
items will spoil, and provide customized recipes using pre-existing ingredients.

## Currently Implemented:

Everything below works on the server. The phone app is the next big piece.

- [x] Accounts and secure sign-in
- [x] Add, edit and remove food items, with where they're kept (fridge, freezer or pantry), how much there is, and when they expire
- [x] Look up a product from its barcode, with results saved so the same product loads instantly next time
- [x] Estimate when food will go off, based on USDA food storage guidelines
- [x] A "use it soon" list of what's closest to going off
- [x] Save your diets and allergies, and check food against them

## Future Features:

- [ ] The frontend mobile app itself
- [ ] Scan barcodes with the phone camera
- [ ] Recipe ideas made from what you already have, respecting your diets and allergies
- [ ] Add food by taking a photo of it, its expiry date, or a receipt
- [ ] A backup product source for barcodes the main one doesn't know
